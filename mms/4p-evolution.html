The framework — 3 rules

1. Staged, not sudden. Each P moves through a sequence (Fix → Build → Convert → Scale → Lead); never jump two steps.

2. Readiness-gated. A P shifts to its next positioning only when the gate is met (quality fixed, coverage reached, measurement built). Premature shifts break trust or waste money.

3. Tied to a KPI. Every shift has one KPI gate and one owner; if the gate stays Red for two reviews, the shift is paused and escalated.

Phase model (aligned to the 5-year strategy)

FY26-27 · Fix & defend

Stabilise quality, fill the 255 DBs, build measurement. Protect the core.

FY27-28 · Build demand

Retailer + electrician expansion, awareness, value brand, smart launch.

FY28-29 · Convert & scale

If a gate is not met, the P stays in its current positioning (no premature shift). This is the guardrail that keeps growth realistic.

How this drives the strategy

The 5-year target is delivered by moving each P one stage at a time: Product (quality → smart) unlocks premium; Place + Promotion (coverage → awareness → electrician) create demand; Price protects the premium while the value brand absorbs price-sensitive buyers; People/Process/Physical evidence make it consistent. Consumers, dealers, retailers and electricians each get the right P at the right stage — which is the ICP differentiation feeding Where/Who/How to win.
