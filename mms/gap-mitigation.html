Gap Mitigation across 4P / 7P — closing the gap to the 5-year strategy (ENOVAR / ALEL)

A repeatable process to find the gap in each lever, fix the root cause, and gate the result against the strategy — not one-off fixes.

The mitigation process

1 · Diagnose

Compare each P against the 5-yr target; quantify the gap (revenue, share, coverage, quality).

2 · Prioritize

Rank by impact on the 5-yr target × urgency. Tag P0 (critical) / P1.

3 · Mitigate

Fix the root cause (not the symptom) with a named owner, action and budget.

4 · Measure

Track the KPI gate; Red → decision (CBDO L1–L3).

5 · Recalibrate

Monthly review; reallocate, escalate or close. Loop back to Diagnose.

Cadence: monthly gap review (owner-led) → quarterly CBDO review. Strategic gaps (value brand, smart range, channel model) escalate to CBDO (L3).

Governance — how a gap becomes an action

Each gap is logged with owner, action, KPI gate and date → tracked in the Decision & Actions flow. If the KPI gate stays Red for two reviews, it escalates one level (L1 → L2 → CBDO L3). If the gap closes, it is retired and the next priority is picked. This keeps mitigation process-driven, not crisis-driven.
