ICP profiles

ALEL-ICP-01 · B2C · Tier 2→1

Tanvir & Nusrat — Progress-Seeking Home Upgraders

Who: ~1.1 Cr HH; 6.9 lakh buyers upgrading homes. Need: modern, reliable, attainable electricals/HAP; room-level upgrading.

Hero: GSS Lumigold/Cherry, AC/DC 12W bulb, gas stove/cooker. Offer: room bundle + seasonal 5–10%, A&P 8.03 Cr. Channel: dealers → retailers.

ALEL-ICP-02 · B2B · Tier 1

Mr. Karim — Growth-Minded Authorised Dealer

Who: 255 DB points; turnover 3–30 Cr/yr. Need: margin/scheme, credit, brand pull, fill rate.

Hero: GSS, MCB/SDB, AC/DC bulb. Offer: slab/scheme 4–10% (~24.13 Cr), signboards. KPI: share of wallet 15%→25%, retention ≥95%.

ALEL-ICP-03 · B2B · Tier 2

Rafiq — Neighbourhood Electrical Retailer

Who: 9,000 of ~60,000 outlets. Need: shelf pull, item-set, display gifts.

Hero: GSS, AC/DC & LED bulbs. Offer: carton/display gifts 3.03 Cr. KPI: numeric 9%→15%→30%.

ALEL-ICP-04 · B2B influencer · Tier 2

Kamal Mistri — Trusted Neighbourhood Electrician

Who: 6,000 of ~2.5 lakh. Need: trustworthy quality, tool kit, recognition.

Hero: MCB/SDB, Piano switch, holders. Offer: Pro Electrician points, meets & tool kit 1.72 Cr. ROI 229% — key lever for ICP-01.

ALEL-ICP-05 · B2C · Tier 3 Seed

Ayon — Gen-Z First Home Setter

Who: ~50 lakh; 52k buyers. Need: affordable, smart-ready, design-led.

Hero: AC/DC bulb; smart lighting (future). Offer: creator codes/starter kits 0.69 Cr. Role: future ICP-01 pipeline.
