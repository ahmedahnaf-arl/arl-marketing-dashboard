The positioning thesis

ENOVAR = accessible-premium electrical & home solutions grounded in dependable performance and the Akij legacy — "Designed for Now · Life Ready প্রতিদিন". The 4P must consistently express this: modern, reliable, attainable, and backed by Akij trust + electrician/dealer programmes.

5-year job: move from a #6 challenger (3.9% share) to a scaled top player (8.7%) without losing the accessible-premium position — i.e., grow distribution and consideration, not discount.

What this means for the strategy (bottomline)

To hit 2,301.9 Cr and 8.7% share, positioning must stay consistent across all 4P: a reliable, modern, accessible-premium brand that is available where homes upgrade, recommended by electricians, and never cheapened by discounting. Growth comes from Place + Promotion (distribution and electrician preference) and Product (quality + smart), while Price protects the premium and a separate value brand absorbs price-led demand.
